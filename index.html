// About Me

Curiosity Meets Precision

I believe the most powerful thing a data scientist can do is ask the right question — and then build the right tool to answer it. Whether it's auditing thousands of hospital records for a national health insurer or evaluating an entire EV market to surface the best value for consumers, I approach every dataset with that same intent.

My background spans electronics engineering, a year in production support at MAR Symantech, and now a master's program in Applied Data Science & AI in Germany. That path has given me something rare: the patience to debug deeply, the engineering mindset to build reliably, and the statistical foundation to interpret honestly.

I'm genuinely fascinated by AI — not as a buzzword, but as a craft. My RAG_reader project is a direct expression of that: I wanted to make documents speak, so I built a system that does exactly that using vector embeddings and LLM APIs.
